Everything you need to know about Vestrado Academy

Are Vestrado Academy courses really 100% free?
Yes! All educational content on Vestrado Academy is completely free. We believe in providing quality trading education without any cost, so you can access our webinars, tutorials, and courses without a subscription or purchase.
How long does it take to complete the beginner forex course?
The beginner track typically takes 0-3 months to complete, depending on your pace. Most learners spend 3-5 hours per week and complete the track in 6-8 weeks. You can learn at your own pace and revisit lessons anytime.
Do I get a certificate after completing courses?
Yes! Upon completing each course, you'll receive a digital certificate that you can download, print, and share on LinkedIn or other professional platforms to showcase your achievement.
Can I get 1-on-1 help if I'm stuck on something?
Absolutely! You can get help through our live chat support, community forums where instructors actively participate, and during live Q&A sessions in webinars. We also have a dedicated Academy support team available 24/5.
Are the webinars live or recorded?
We offer both! Live webinars provide real-time interaction with expert traders and Q&A opportunities. All live sessions are also recorded and made available in your account, so you can watch them anytime at your convenience.
