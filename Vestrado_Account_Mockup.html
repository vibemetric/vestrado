What is Dynamic Leverage and how does it protect me?

Dynamic Leverage automatically reduces as your account balance grows to protect you from excessive risk.

Example:

  • $0 - $5,000 balance: Up to 1:2000 leverage
  • $5,000 - $20,000: Reduces to 1:500
  • $20,000 - $100,000: Reduces to 1:200
  • $100,000+: Reduces to 1:100

Why? High leverage is riskier with larger accounts. A 1% loss on $100,000 with 1:2000 leverage could mean $200,000 loss position. Dynamic leverage prevents this.

💡 Beginner tip: Even though 1:2000 is available, we recommend starting with 1:100 or lower while you learn. You can always increase it later.
