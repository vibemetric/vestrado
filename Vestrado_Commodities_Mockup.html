⚠️ Commodity Trading Risk Disclosure

Important: Trading commodity CFDs carries specific risks beyond typical Forex trading:

  • Price Volatility: Commodity prices can fluctuate dramatically due to geopolitical events (wars, sanctions), weather patterns (hurricanes affecting oil production), and supply disruptions (OPEC cuts).
  • Leverage Risk: While leverage up to 1:500 (metals) and 1:200 (energies) can amplify profits, it equally amplifies losses. A 2% adverse price move with 1:500 leverage results in a 1000% loss of margin.
  • Rollover Costs: Commodity CFDs are based on futures contracts. When contracts expire (monthly for oil, quarterly for others), positions are rolled over, which may incur additional costs.
  • Market Gaps: Commodities can gap significantly over weekends or during major news events (e.g., OPEC meetings, Fed announcements), potentially triggering stop losses or causing slippage.
  • Correlation Changes: Historical correlations (e.g., gold vs USD) can break down during market crises, making hedging strategies ineffective.
