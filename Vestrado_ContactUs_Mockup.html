Quick Answers to Common Questions

Find instant answers to the most frequently asked questions

How do I reset my password?
Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your registered email, and you'll receive a reset link within minutes. Make sure to check your spam folder if you don't see it.
What are your withdrawal fees and processing times?
We don't charge withdrawal fees for most methods. Bank transfers take 1-3 business days, e-wallets are processed within 24 hours, and crypto withdrawals are completed within 1 hour.
How long does account verification take?
Most accounts are verified within 24 hours on business days. Make sure all documents are clear and valid. You can start trading with a Demo account while waiting for verification.
Can I change my leverage after opening an account?
Yes! You can adjust your leverage anytime through your account dashboard. Please note that higher leverage increases both potential profits and risks.
What's the minimum deposit to start trading?
Our minimum deposit is just $50 for a Standard account. However, we recommend starting with at least $200 to have better risk management and trading flexibility.
Do you offer a demo account?
Absolutely! We offer free demo accounts with $10,000 virtual money. It's perfect for testing strategies and learning the platform. No credit card required!
