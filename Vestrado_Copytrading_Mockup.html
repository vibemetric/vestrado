Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Copy Trading at Vestrado.

How does Copy Trading work? ▼
When you copy a trader, their trades are automatically replicated in your account in real-time. You maintain full control and can stop copying anytime with one click. You choose the copy amount and ratio.
Can I stop copying immediately? ▼
Yes. There is NO lock-in period. Click "Stop Copying" and you can either: (1) Close all copied trades immediately, or (2) Keep current positions open but stop new trades from being copied. It's your choice.
How are traders verified? ▼
All Strategy Providers must complete KYC verification, maintain a 6+ month track record, and have at least 50 copiers. We monitor all accounts 24/7 for suspicious activity and auto-suspend any trader showing unusual patterns.
What are the fees for Copy Trading? ▼
Copy Trading is FREE to use. There are NO performance fees (unlike competitors who charge 20%). You pay the same spreads as standard accounts (from 0.0 pip). Minimum copy amount is $100 per trader.
Is past performance a guarantee of future results? ▼
No. Past performance is not indicative of future results. All traders can experience losses. Copy Trading involves significant risk, and 76.4% of retail accounts lose money. Only invest what you can afford to lose.
Can I copy multiple traders at once? ▼
Yes. You can copy up to 100 traders simultaneously. This allows you to diversify across different strategies, risk levels, and asset classes to balance your portfolio.

⚠️ Important Risk Disclosure

Copy Trading involves significant risk of loss. When you copy a trader, you are following their strategy which may include high-leverage positions and volatile instruments. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

Key Risks:

  • You can lose some or all of your invested capital
  • Copied traders may experience losing streaks
  • Network delays may cause slight differences in execution
  • High-risk traders (7-10/10) can lose significant amounts quickly
  • No trader can guarantee profits or avoid losses

Before you start: Only invest money you can afford to lose. Start with low-risk traders (1-3/10) if you're new. Use the Demo Account to test the service without risking real money. Read the full Terms & Conditions.
