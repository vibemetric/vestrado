Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about crypto trading with Vestrado

How secure is my cryptocurrency with Vestrado? ▼
We store 95% of user crypto assets in offline cold storage (air-gapped hardware wallets) in bank-grade vaults. Only 5% is kept in hot wallets for instant withdrawals. All large transactions require multi-signature approval (3-of-5). Your funds are further protected by $100M insurance through Lloyd's of London. We are SOC 2 Type II certified and undergo regular security audits.
What's the difference between trading Bitcoin Spot vs Bitcoin CFD? ▼
Spot Trading: You buy and own actual Bitcoin. You can withdraw it to your external wallet anytime. Best for long-term holding. No leverage available.

CFD Trading: You speculate on Bitcoin's price movement without owning it. You can use leverage (up to 1:100) and short sell. Cannot withdraw actual BTC, only cash settle profit/loss. Best for active trading.
Why is crypto more volatile than forex? ▼
Cryptocurrencies have smaller market caps than major fiat currencies, making them more susceptible to large price swings. Crypto markets operate 24/7/365 with no circuit breakers. Major news, whale movements, or regulatory announcements can cause 10-20% moves in hours. Additionally, crypto lacks central bank intervention that stabilizes traditional currencies. This volatility creates both opportunities and risks - leverage amplifies both.
Can I see my crypto transactions on the blockchain? ▼
Yes! For Spot Trading accounts, every deposit and withdrawal transaction is recorded on the blockchain. We provide you with the Transaction ID (Tx Hash) which you can verify on public block explorers like Etherscan (for Ethereum) or Blockchain.com (for Bitcoin). This ensures complete transparency. For CFD trading, transactions are cash-settled off-chain, similar to traditional trading.
What is your crypto leverage policy? ▼
We offer tiered leverage based on asset liquidity: Bitcoin (BTC) up to 1:100, Ethereum (ETH) up to 1:50, major altcoins (XRP, BNB, ADA) up to 1:20, and small-cap altcoins up to 1:10. Higher leverage means higher liquidation risk. We strongly recommend starting with lower leverage (1:5 or 1:10) until you fully understand crypto volatility. Use our Liquidation Calculator to see your exact risk level before trading.
