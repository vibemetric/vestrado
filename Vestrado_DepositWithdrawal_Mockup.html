Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Quick answers to common deposit & withdrawal questions

How long does it take for my deposit to reflect in my account?

Most deposits (Local Bank Transfer, Credit Card, USDT, E-Wallets, Cryptocurrencies) are processed instantly within 5-10 minutes during business hours. Wire transfers take 2-7 business days. Deposits made after 5 PM GMT or on weekends are processed the next business day.

Are there any fees for deposits or withdrawals?

Vestrado does NOT charge any deposit fees (0%). However, external fees may apply: your bank may charge currency conversion fees (0.5-1%), card issuers may charge cash advance fees (2-3%), and crypto networks charge gas fees. For withdrawals, most methods are also 0% except Cryptocurrencies (1% processing fee + network fees). We cover wire transfer fees. All fees are disclosed upfront in our payment tables above.

How do I withdraw funds from my trading account?

Log in to your Vestrado account → Click "Withdraw" → Select your withdrawal method → Enter amount and bank/wallet details → Confirm with 2FA → Check email for confirmation link → Click to approve. Our Finance Team will process your request within the indicated timeframe (typically 1-24 hours for verified accounts).

What documents do I need to withdraw?

Before your first withdrawal, you must complete KYC verification by providing: 1) Government-issued ID (Passport or National ID), 2) Proof of Address (utility bill or bank statement less than 3 months old), and 3) Payment Method Verification (for card: photo of card front with middle 8 digits masked; for bank: proof of account ownership). Verification typically takes 1-2 business days.

How long does account verification take?

Standard verification takes 1-2 business days once you submit all required documents. Our compliance team reviews during business hours (Mon-Fri 9 AM - 5 PM GMT). For urgent cases, contact support@vestrado.com with subject "Priority Verification" – we'll do our best to expedite.

Can I withdraw without verification?

No. Due to international AML/KYC regulations, all withdrawals require completed account verification. This protects both you and Vestrado from fraud. You can deposit without verification, but we strongly recommend verifying your account immediately to enable instant withdrawals when you're ready.

How do you protect my withdrawal requests?

We use multiple security layers: 1) Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) required for all requests, 2) Email Confirmation – you must click a link in your email to approve, 3) IP Verification – requests from new locations trigger extra checks, 4) 24-Hour Cooling Period for large withdrawals (>USD 5,000), and 5) AI Fraud Detection monitoring 24/7.

What if someone tries to withdraw my funds?

Even if someone gains access to your account, they cannot withdraw funds without: 1) Your 2FA code (on your mobile device), 2) Access to your email to click confirmation link, and 3) Matching bank account name to your registered name. If suspicious activity is detected, our system automatically freezes withdrawals and notifies you immediately via email and SMS.

Do I need 2FA for withdrawals?

Yes, 2FA is mandatory for all withdrawal requests to protect your funds. You can set it up in Account Settings → Security → Enable 2FA. We support Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, and SMS-based 2FA. This takes 2 minutes to set up and adds critical security to your account.

Why does my credit card withdrawal take 3-5 days?

Credit card withdrawals take longer because they're processed as refunds by your card issuer, not direct transfers. Vestrado processes your request within 24 hours, but your bank's refund system requires 2-4 additional business days for the funds to appear in your card account. This is a standard banking limitation, not a Vestrado delay. For faster withdrawals, consider Local Bank Transfer (1-24 hours) or USDT (1-24 hours).

Can I deposit in my local currency?

Yes! We support multiple local currencies through Local Bank Transfer: MYR (Malaysian Ringgit), IDR (Indonesian Rupiah), THB (Thai Baht), SGD (Singapore Dollar), VND (Vietnamese Dong), INR (Indian Rupee), BRL (Brazilian Real), and more. Your deposit will be converted to USD at mid-market rate + 0.5% markup (transparent pricing). Use our Fee Calculator above to see exact conversion rates.

What if I need to deposit more than USD 10,000?

For deposits over USD 10,000: 1) Contact our VIP Banking Team at vip@vestrado.com or call +357-XXX-XXXX, 2) We offer preferential rates for large deposits, 3) Wire Transfer up to USD 5 million available with dedicated account manager, 4) Higher daily limits for verified VIP accounts (up to USD 500,000/day). We'll guide you through the entire process personally.
