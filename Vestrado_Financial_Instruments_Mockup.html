IMPORTANT: CFD Ownership Disclosure

Share CFDs do NOT give you ownership of the underlying shares. You are trading on price movements only, not buying actual stocks. You will not receive dividends, voting rights, or shareholder benefits. CFDs are leveraged products with wider spreads than direct stock purchases (typical spread: 0.1%-0.5% vs 0.001% for stocks).

Share CFD Risk Considerations

  • Spreads are wider than Forex (0.1%-0.5% vs 0.0001%)
  • Commission charged on both entry and exit (0.08% each way = 0.16% total)
  • Limited leverage (1:5 retail) means larger capital requirement
  • Best for: Gaining stock market exposure with leverage, not long-term investing
