What is Forex Trading?

Forex (Foreign Exchange) is the world's largest financial market where currencies are traded 24/5. You profit from currency pair price movements - buying low and selling high. With leverage, you can control larger positions with smaller capital. However, leverage also increases risk, which is why we recommend starting with a Demo Account to practice risk-free.
