⚠️ Important Notes on Indices Trading

  • Contract Size Explanation: For Indices, 1 lot = Contract Size × Index Value. Example: 1 lot US30 @ 35,000 points = $35,000 exposure per point move.
  • Margin Calculation: Margin 1% = Maximum Leverage 1:100. At 1% margin, 1 lot US30 requires approximately $350 margin (varies with market price).
  • Spreads May Widen: During major news releases (Fed announcements, NFP data, market open), spreads may temporarily increase by 2-5x.
  • Leverage Risk: High leverage magnifies both profits AND losses. 1% price move with 1:100 leverage = 100% account impact.
  • Weekend Margins: Margin requirements may increase 2x from Friday close to Sunday open to protect against weekend gap risk.

❓ Common Questions About Indices Trading

Q: What's the minimum position size?
A: 0.01 lots (micro lots). On US30, this equals $0.01 per point move - perfect for beginners.

Q: Do I pay overnight fees on index trades?
A: No overnight fees if you open and close positions on the same day. For positions held overnight, a small swap fee applies (varies by index).

Q: Can I trade indices on weekends?
A: No. Indices trade Sunday 23:00 GMT to Friday 22:00 GMT with a 1-hour daily break at 22:00-23:00 GMT.

Q: What happens during rollover?
A: We automatically roll your position to the next futures contract. Spreads may widen 48 hours before expiry. You'll receive email notification 1 week in advance.
