❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MetaTrader 5

How do I install MetaTrader 5 on my computer? +
Download the MT5 installer from our download buttons above. Run the .exe file (Windows) or .dmg file (Mac), follow the on-screen instructions, and click "Install". The entire process takes about 2 minutes. After installation, open MT5, search for "Vestrado" in the server list, and login with your credentials.
Is MetaTrader 5 free to download and use? +
Yes, MetaTrader 5 is completely free software developed by MetaQuotes. There are no download fees, no subscription fees, and no hidden costs. You only need to fund your Vestrado trading account when you're ready to start live trading.
Can I use Expert Advisors (EAs) on Vestrado MT5? +
Yes! Vestrado MT5 fully supports Expert Advisors (automated trading robots). You can code your own EAs in MQL5, purchase them from the MQL5 Market, or use free EAs from the community. EAs can run 24/7 if you use VPS hosting.
What's the difference between MT5 Desktop and WebTrader? +
MT5 Desktop is a full-featured application installed on your computer with all advanced tools (EAs, backtesting, custom indicators). WebTrader runs in your browser with no installation required—perfect for quick trades on shared computers or when traveling. Both platforms access the same Vestrado account.
Can I sync my MT5 across multiple devices? +
Yes! Your Vestrado MT5 account syncs automatically across all devices via cloud. Charts, indicators, watchlists, and open positions are accessible from Desktop, Mobile, and WebTrader. Just login with the same credentials on each device.
How fast is trade execution on Vestrado MT5? +
Vestrado's MT5 servers are located in London Equinix LD4 Data Center with average execution speed under 0.01 seconds (10 milliseconds). 99% of market orders are executed with zero slippage, and we guarantee no requotes on market orders.
