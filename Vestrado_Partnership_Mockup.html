How Your Earnings Work

Our commission structure is designed to reward your success. The more clients you refer and the higher their trading volumes, the more you earn. Here's what you need to know:

Per-Lot Rebates

Earn a fixed rebate for every lot your clients trade. Standard partners start at $12/lot, scaling up to $22/lot for Gold partners.

Volume Bonuses

Reach higher monthly volume thresholds to unlock increased rebate rates and tier upgrades automatically.

Sub-IB Revenue

Gold and Platinum partners earn additional commission when they refer other partners (Sub-IBs) who generate trades.

Instant Crediting

Commissions are calculated and credited to your partner wallet instantly after each client trade closes. No delays.

If you're a Silver Partner and your clients trade 1,200 lots in a month:

Your Earnings = 1,200 lots × $15/lot = $18,000/month

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How much can I earn as a Vestrado partner?
Earnings vary based on your marketing efforts and referred client trading volumes. Some partners earn $500/month, others $50,000+. Your potential is unlimited, but income is not guaranteed and past performance is not indicative of future results. Commission rates range from $12-22/lot depending on your tier.
How fast do I get paid?
Commissions are calculated and credited to your partner wallet instantly after each client trade closes. You can withdraw anytime with a minimum balance of $50. Withdrawals typically process within 1-24 hours depending on your payment method.
Are there any fees to become a partner?
No. There are zero upfront fees, monthly fees, or hidden costs to join the Vestrado Partnership Programme. You only earn commission when your referred clients trade. Withdrawal fees apply based on your chosen payout method (detailed in payment section).
What marketing support do I get?
All partners receive access to 200+ marketing materials including banners, landing pages, email templates, and social media content in 10 languages. Gold and Platinum partners receive additional custom marketing materials and dedicated account manager support for campaign optimization.
Can I track my referrals and earnings in real-time?
Yes. Your partner dashboard updates every 60 seconds with live commission totals, client registration tracking, trading activity, and detailed analytics. You can access your dashboard 24/7 from any device including our mobile app.
What are the risks of becoming a partner?
Partnership is performance-based. Your earnings depend entirely on client acquisition and trading activity. There is no base salary or guaranteed income. Market conditions, your marketing effectiveness, and client behavior all impact your earnings. You are responsible for compliance with local marketing and tax regulations. Review our complete Risk Disclosure before applying.
