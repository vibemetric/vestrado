🔄 Volume Requirements & Calculation

+

How volume is calculated:

  • Volume = Total lot size traded (buy + sell)
  • 1 standard lot = 100,000 units of base currency
  • Example: Trade 1 lot EUR/USD = $100,000 volume
  • Partial lot example: 0.5 lot = $50,000 volume
  • Both winning AND losing trades count toward volume requirement
  • All instruments accepted: Forex, Crypto, Commodities, Indices, Shares
  • Check your progress in Dashboard → Bonuses → Volume Tracker

❌ Prohibited Activities

+

The following will result in bonus cancellation and potential account closure:

  • Bonus abuse: Opening multiple accounts to claim bonuses multiple times
  • Hedging: Opening opposite positions on the same instrument across multiple accounts
  • Arbitrage trading using bonus funds
  • Scalping with holding periods under 3 minutes (on bonus-funded trades)
  • Using third-party EAs/bots designed to exploit bonus conditions
  • Colluding with other traders to manipulate volume requirements
  • Making deposits and withdrawals without trading (deposit cycling)
