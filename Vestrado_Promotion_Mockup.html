Can I withdraw my bonus immediately? +

No, bonuses are locked until you complete the required trading volume (typically 20-30x your deposit). However, you can withdraw your original deposit and any profits from trading at any time. The bonus unlocks once volume requirements are met.

What's the difference between my deposit and the bonus? +

Your Deposit: 100% yours, withdrawable anytime.
Bonus Credit: Locked until volume met, used for trading power but cannot be withdrawn (only profits from it).
Trading Profits: 100% yours, withdrawable anytime.

Can I claim multiple bonuses at once? +

You can only have ONE active deposit bonus at a time (e.g., can't combine 100% Welcome + 50% Reload). However, cashback and trading contests are stackable with deposit bonuses.

What happens if I don't meet the volume requirement? +

If you don't complete the required volume within the validity period (typically 60-90 days), the bonus will expire and be removed from your account. Your deposit and profits remain yours and can still be withdrawn.

Is the $30 no deposit bonus real money? +
